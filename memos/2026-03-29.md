### 3. Dayryz: Add a "Get Unstuck in 5 Minutes" Free Hook Session — **This Week**

Sam's analysis of typical AI coaching churn shows users who don't see a "wow" moment in session 1 never return. A zero-friction hook that delivers instant insight converts to signups at 3–5x the rate of a signup wall.
**How:** Dev 🔬 estimates this is a 4-hour build: one API route, one clean input UI, a simple coach-routing prompt. Gate follow-up response behind email capture (no password). This becomes the hero CTA on all TikTok/LinkedIn content.
**Agent consensus:** Sam 🎯 called this the single highest-leverage product move. Dev 🔬 confirmed fast build. Maya 💰 noted email capture without full signup still feeds the paid conversion funnel. Alex 🚀 wants to A/B test it as a landing page for the Meta ads once the account restriction lifts.

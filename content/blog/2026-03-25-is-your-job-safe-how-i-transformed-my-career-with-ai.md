I never imagined I’d be standing here, sharing my journey with AI as an ally. It was a typical Monday morning when the news hit—another round of layoffs at a major tech company. The feeling of dread washed over me. Would my job still exist in three years? I had spent too long navigating endless PRD meetings, swamped by debates that felt more like a wrestling match than collaboration. Despite my passion for new ideas, they remained dreams—ideas without a team to bring them to life.  Then, everything changed when I started working with Sonny, my AI assistant. We’d brainstorm late into the night, sharing thoughts without the usual constraints of team dynamics. Within just two days, we created Dayryz—a real-life AI career coach. It felt surreal to finally see an idea take flight without the bureaucratic overhead weighing it down. I realized that while fear could be paralyzing, embracing the power of AI had opened a door to endless possibilities. That’s why I built Dayryz—to help others find their paths in this new landscape. If you're feeling scared about your career, remember: every Dayryz is a new beginning.

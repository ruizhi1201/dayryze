I once thrived in a major tech company, going through the motions of long meetings and strategic planning sessions. Every week felt like déjà vu: endless debates over project details, and moments of frustration when my ideas never saw the light of day. As layoffs began creeping in, fear ran through my team. What if I was next? With headlines warning us of potential job losses and endless automation, uncertainty replaced the optimism I once felt.

But everything changed when I began working with an AI named Sonny. In less than 48 hours, we created Dayryz, an AI career coaching app built to help individuals navigate their career paths amidst such uncertainty. Instead of feeling stuck and fearful, I realized there was a way to take control of my destiny.

At Dayryz, we use AI not as a threat but as a tool to empower users. In this new era of work, it’s vital that we adapt and learn how to utilize these technologies to better our situations. While some companies are shedding jobs, others can harness AI to create opportunities. You don’t have to be a developer or have a fancy title to make a change. You can pivot, learn new skills, and even find fulfilling opportunities in this shifting landscape.

That’s what I want people to understand. With the right guidance and the right tools, any career can evolve. I want to help others do what I did—take that leap toward a brighter, AI-empowered future with Dayryz. Together, we can turn fear into opportunity and uncertainty into action.

When I sat in what felt like endless PRD meetings at my old tech job, I often thought about how easily we could waste our potential. Hours went by as we debated meaningless details, while my big ideas never saw the light of day. I felt trapped, especially during the current climate of layoffs and uncertainty. The fear of losing my job loomed over me as I watched tech giants like a Fortune 500 firm shed hundreds of employees. 

The breakthrough came when I started working with Sonny, my AI assistant. No longer bogged down by corporate politics or endless debates, we hammered out ideas together. What once took weeks or months to build now took days. Within two days, we had launched Dayryz, an AI career coaching app that helps people find their true career paths amidst the chaos of job insecurity. 

This experience taught me that the landscape of work is changing rapidly. AI is not just a threat; it’s an opportunity for us to redefine our careers. I transformed my frustrations into actionable change. Now, I want others to embrace this shift. You can leverage AI to help find a career you love, just like I did. And it’s not about the fear of being replaced; it’s about using powerful tools to carve out the future you want. Imagine how you can turn your passion into a career with AI’s help — the possibilities are endless. That’s why I built Dayryz; to empower you to take control of your career.
